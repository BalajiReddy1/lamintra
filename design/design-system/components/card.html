Card

A surface that groups related content. Radius 24 — the largest step in the scale, where the superellipse is most visible. Padding is 20 on all sides; media bleeds to the edge and is clipped by the card's own shape, which is the clearest demonstration of the geometry we've committed to.

Outlined

A hairline boundary. The default — quiet enough to repeat down a list without the page getting noisy.

Elevated

A soft shadow plus a 1px top keyline, so the surface catches light instead of just casting a blur.

Slots — media, body, actions

Content is composed from slots rather than fixed title/subtitle parameters, so a caller can put anything in a card without us anticipating it. Media sits flush to the edges; body and actions keep the 20 inset.

Interactive

The whole surface is pressable. Press is 0.985, not the button's 0.97 — a large surface scaling as hard as a small one looks rubbery.

pressable card — scale tuned to surface size

Concentric radii — the rule most systems get wrong

When one rounded shape sits inside another, the inner radius must equal the outer radius minus the padding (24 − 16 = 8). Reuse the same radius for both and the curves stop being parallel; the gap visibly pinches at the corners. It is a small rule that quietly separates considered work from assembled work.

onClick is nullable: null gives a plain surface, non-null makes the whole card pressable with the correct 48dp-minimum semantics and press feedback. One component rather than Card + ClickableCard, because the difference is behaviour, not identity.
