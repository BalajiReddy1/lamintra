# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be straightforward and assume good faith. **Criticism of the product is welcome
and wanted** - this project's own documents are unusually blunt about what does
not work yet, and you are held to that same standard, not a politer one.

What is not welcome is aimed at people rather than work.

## What that means

**Fine, and encouraged:**

- "This does not work and here is exactly how I broke it."
- "I looked at this and decided not to use it, because..."
- "This design decision is wrong, and here is the reason."
- Being blunt, brief, or unimpressed.

**Not fine:**

- Personal attacks, insults, or demeaning comments.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

Applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and any space where
you are representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report anything that needs attention by opening an issue titled "Conduct" with
no details in it, and a private channel will be arranged - or use GitHub's
[report abuse](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) form for anything
serious or urgent.

This is a single-maintainer project. Reports are handled by the maintainer, who
will keep the reporter's identity private. Responses range from a request to
edit a comment, through removing content, to blocking an account from the
repository. There is no committee and no appeals process - that would be
inventing structure this project does not have.

## Attribution
